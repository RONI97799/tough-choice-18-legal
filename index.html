Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This page explains what information the game "Tough Choice: Adults Edition" keeps, why, and what you can do about it. The game is run by R.B.A, operated by Roni Ben Avi, Israel ("we", "us").

1. In short

You do not need to sign up to play. We do not ask for, or keep, your real name, email, phone number, address, location, contacts or photos.

We keep only what the game needs in order to work: the nickname you chose, your answers, and your streak in the question of the day.

You can erase all of it at any time from inside the game: Settings → Delete my data.

2. What is kept

An anonymous player ID: a random number created automatically the first time you open the game. It says nothing about you.

Nickname: the name you chose to show friends in a room. Do not use your full name.

Answers: which side you picked on each question, to show percentages and avoid repeating questions.

Question of the day: when you answered, and your current and best streak.

Rooms: the questions you wrote in a room, your picks, guesses and score. A room is deleted automatically a few hours after play ends.

Game totals: how many room games you played, how many you won and how many points you scored.

Reports: if you reported a question or a nickname, or were reported, the report and the reported text are kept so we can review it, until reviewed and for no longer than 6 months.

On your device only: your settings (language, music, sounds, vibration and reminder).

3. Why we use it

To make the game work: showing questions and percentages, running rooms, counting scores and streaks.

To keep the game pleasant: filtering offensive words and handling reports.

We do not sell information and do not use it to learn who you are.

4. Ads

The game shows full-screen ads between rounds through Google AdMob, no more than one every three minutes. The ads are not personalised: we ask Google not to choose ads by tracking you, and we limit ads to content suitable for adults (MA rating), with no gambling ads.

To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, the AdMob library sends Google, directly from the phone: your IP address (and from it an approximate location, at city or country level), device and system details, the device advertising ID, and information about the ads shown and tapped. This data does not pass through us and we have no access to it. Google handles it under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone settings.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Union and the UK), Google shows a window before ads where you choose whether to consent, and keeps your choice.

On an iPhone, the game asks Apple's "Allow tracking" question once, before ads start. The ads stay non-personalised whatever you choose, and the game works the same. If you allow tracking on iPhone, Google may use the advertising ID to measure ads and prevent fraud, so Apple's store lists this data as 'used to track you'.

5. Where it is kept and who sees it

The information is stored on Supabase servers in Europe (Frankfurt, Germany).

Game updates are delivered through Expo, which receives technical details about the device and the game version for that purpose.

Your nickname and the questions you wrote in a room are shown only to the players in that room with you.

We do not pass information to anyone else unless the law requires us to.

The server is shared with our other game, "Tough Choice" (all ages), but each game's data is kept separately: each game has its own tables and functions, and players of one game cannot see or change the other game's data.

6. Adults only (18+)

The game is for ages 18 and up only. On first launch it asks for your month and year of birth to check your age. The date is not sent to us and is not kept: the phone keeps only the answer (18 or over, or not).

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is playing or has written a personal detail, write to us and we will erase the data. All data can also be erased from inside the game.

7. How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Information is kept for as long as you play. Rooms are deleted automatically within a few hours.

Full deletion: Settings → Delete my data. Your nickname, answers, streak and totals are erased from the server at once and cannot be restored.

You can also ask for deletion by email.

8. Notifications

If you allowed it, the game sends a daily reminder for the question of the day. The reminder is scheduled on the device itself and can be turned off in the game's settings or the phone's settings.

9. Your rights

Legal basis: game data, on our server and on your phone, is kept so the game works (performing the service you asked for). Ads are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding a free game and, where the law requires it, on the basis of your consent in Google's window.

Under the law that applies to you (for example Israel's Privacy Protection Law or the GDPR), you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Most of these rights can be used from inside the game with "Delete my data" (section 7), or by writing to us. For data collected for ads, you can contact Google or change your choice in the consent window.

You can also complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, or to the data protection authority in your country.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, the updated version will appear here with the new date.

Questions and requests: r.b.a.app977@gmail.com

2. איזה מידע נשמר

מזהה שחקן אנונימי: מספר אקראי שנוצר אוטומטית בפעם הראשונה שפותחים את המשחק. הוא לא מכיל שום פרט עליכם.

כינוי: השם שבחרתם להציג לחברים בחדר. אל תשתמשו בשם המלא שלכם.

תשובות: באיזה צד בחרתם בכל שאלה, כדי להציג אחוזים ולא לחזור על שאלות.

שאלת היום: מתי עניתם, ומה הרצף והשיא שלכם.

חדרים: השאלות שכתבתם בחדר, הבחירות, הניחושים והניקוד. חדר נמחק אוטומטית כמה שעות אחרי שמסיימים לשחק בו.

סיכום משחקים: כמה משחקי חדר שיחקתם, כמה ניצחתם וכמה נקודות צברתם.

דיווחים: אם דיווחתם על שאלה או כינוי, או שדיווחו עליכם, הדיווח והטקסט שדווח נשמרים כדי שנוכל לבדוק אותו, עד שנבדוק ולא יותר מ-6 חודשים.

במכשיר עצמו בלבד נשמרות ההגדרות שלכם: שפה, מוזיקה, צלילים, רטט ותזכורת.

כדי שהמשחק יעבוד: להציג שאלות ואחוזים, לנהל חדרים, לספור ניקוד ורצף.

כדי לשמור על משחק נעים: לסנן מילים פוגעניות ולטפל בדיווחים.

אנחנו לא מוכרים מידע, ולא משתמשים בו כדי להכיר אתכם אישית.
